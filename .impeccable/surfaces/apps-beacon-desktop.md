Scope: the Kanna Beacon desktop app (apps/beacon-desktop): one small window plus a tray icon. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience: a non-technical owner of a Windows or macOS computer who wants their Kanna agent to reach files there. Task: pair once from a link, choose folders and whether commands may run, then trust what happens. Constraint: Kanna's established world (DESIGN.md) binds; the grant is default-deny and every consent is explicit; vi and en by OS locale.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The window is this computer's record of what Kanna read and ran here, with the grant written above it. It refuses the preferences-pane-of-toggles arrangement every daemon ships.

OWN-WORLD: Kanna's four inks on Warm Paper; Body for prose, Roboto Mono for times, paths and commands; shape-first state marks (doubled, based, struck, half); hairline rules between regions, no cards, no shadows; Coral only for unpair and refused.

STORY: The owner clicks the link in Kanna, confirms the address, picks folders, and reads the grant as a sentence. Later they open the window to see exactly what was read or run, and pause or unpair in one click.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Status line at the top (mark, state word, Kanna host, machine name). Under it the grant: two label rows (May read, Commands) with Change. Then the record, newest first, grouped by day, each row time, verb, target, outcome mark. Footer: Pause and an overflow menu.

FORM: The record; candidate 3 of the ordered list; seed key 37a21e97. Build path code-led (decision taken through the text channel, no comps).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
