On a phone, motion is the only navigation you have

Below 768px Kanna already swaps to one column: the sidebar becomes a full-screen overlay you can swipe in from the left edge, the status row shrinks to a pill, and the composer’s preference pills collapse into a Chat settings dialog. All of that is real today — what’s missing is that every one of those changes is instant, so the phone gives you no sense of where anything went.
