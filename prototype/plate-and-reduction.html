Plates, not cards

A transcript entry is space, one hairline, and a caption that states a fact. No box, no radius, no shadow, no nesting. The saved chrome is what pays for the white, and it is where the brief's negative space actually comes from.

The verbatim exception

The four inks govern the chrome. They do not govern what the agent actually produced. A diff, a terminal buffer, a stack trace is the preserved photograph. It keeps its own truth, and additions and deletions keep their colour, because that is how every developer already reads them.
